1. Decision problem

Payoffs are benefits (higher is better). Rows are alternatives, columns are states of nature. Use the buttons to add or remove either.

2. Criteria and recommendations

Regret table

★ marks the best value for each criterion. Ties are all highlighted. Regret = best payoff in that state minus the payoff obtained.

3. Staged filtering

Stage 1 removes dominated alternatives. Stages 2 and 3 apply the thresholds above (leave blank to skip). Stage 4 keeps the highest EMV among survivors. Regret is measured on the full table. “—” means already eliminated in an earlier stage.

4. Flow diagram

Each box is one stage of the filtering. Struck-through names were removed at that stage. The highlighted box is the final outcome.
